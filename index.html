SERVICES

What we do

From monthly bookkeeping to fractional CFO engagements, every service is delivered with senior-level attention.

Bookkeeping

Monthly bookkeeping to keep your records clean, current, and audit-ready. Includes reconciliations, financial statements, and a monthly check-in.

Catch-Up & Clean-Up

Behind on your books? We'll get you current — whether it's a few months or a few years. Ideal for growing businesses and new clients switching providers.

Grant Reporting & Fund Accounting

Specialized reporting for nonprofits and grant-funded organizations. Ensures compliance with funder requirements and supports audit readiness.

Financial Advisory

On-demand guidance for financial decisions, planning, and reporting. A sounding board when you need CFO-level thinking without the full-time cost.

Gig Worker Tax Management

Quarterly estimated tax planning, Schedule C preparation, and year-round bookkeeping for freelancers and self-employed individuals.

Fractional CFO

Strategic financial leadership on a part-time basis. Cash flow forecasting, scenario modeling, board reporting, and financial oversight — when you need it.

ABOUT

Finance experience that travels.

Larry Edward Williams II is a finance and accounting professional with senior-level experience across small businesses, nonprofits, and international organizations. He founded LEW Advisory to give Charlottesville-area clients access to the same quality of financial thinking that large institutions take for granted.

Most recently based in Berlin, Larry managed multi-entity, donor-funded program finances at the International Civil Society Centre. He holds a B.Sc. in Business Management & Administration (Finance) from Touro University and is a QuickBooks ProAdvisor.
